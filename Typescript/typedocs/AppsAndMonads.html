Apps and monads in the registry ​

The registry (runtime/apps.json, listed by GET /apps) holds two kinds of entry, and they are not the same thing.

monadapp
What it isthe runtime that belongs to a .me: identity, claims, the treewhat people open: a Vite dev server, a built dist behind a static server, any server
Who reports itthe monad itself (every 3 s)the app, or netget attach on its behalf
Entry idmonad:<uuid>app:<name>
Gatemust answer /__surface with a monadIdmust answer HTTP on a loopback port
Trustderived from the identity it declaresalways guest
Can be stopped by netgetonly what netget spawned itself, and only with a signed proof + the capabilitynever

Two entries can share a name (and a port: a monad that also serves a dist). They stay two entries.

Reporting an app ​

bash
netget attach fulltrailer --port 4100 --monad fulltrailer --entry dist --title FullTrailer
netget attach demo --port 5173 --entry vite
netget attach demo --port 5173 --once        # report once; the entry expires after its ttl (12 s)
netget detach demo                           # take it out now

attach reports every 3 s until it is stopped; Ctrl-C releases the entry. Against another gateway: NETGET_LOCAL=http://host (and NETGET_LOCAL_HOST=localhost when the gateway answers its admin block on that name).

On the wire (POST /apps/report, loopback only): { "kind": "app", "name": "<name>", "port": <n>, "metadata": { "title", "monad", "entry" } }. Only those fields are kept. A directEndpoint, claimed_namespaces, an identity hash or a host in the report are dropped: an app's own word is not evidence of who it is, and nothing an app sends makes it the monad of a host.

What apps do and do not do in routing ​

  • app:<name> (a declared route, or the app behind a door) resolves to the app entry when one is live under that name, and to the monad of that name otherwise. A route that worked before an app was reported keeps working.
  • Selection by what monads declare (their namespace, aliases, hosts) and by the authorized root only reads monad entries.
  • /apps/<name>/... (monad_proxy) only reaches monads.
  • A door connection may point at an app's own server (its port is not refused); a monad's port still is.

Proven in tests/gateway-app-kind.test.ts (what the gateway accepts and refuses, restart-all, routing) and tests/gateway-app-attach.test.ts (what attach sends, the heartbeat, the CLI), on a disposable OpenResty.
